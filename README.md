# From Farm to Shelf: Australian Banana Supply Chain Risk Analysis

An Excel-based portfolio project examining Australian banana production, regional concentration and how severe weather can disrupt the fresh-produce supply chain.

## Project overview

Bananas are an important everyday product in Australian retail. However, most domestic production is concentrated in Queensland, particularly Far North Queensland. This creates a supply-chain exposure: severe rainfall, flooding and transport disruption in key growing regions can affect harvesting, farm access and the movement of produce to market.

This project brings together production, regional and weather information to explore that exposure. It is an independent analysis using publicly available data.

## Questions explored

- How has Australian banana production changed over the latest five-year period in the dataset?
- How concentrated is production across states and key growing regions?
- What does this concentration mean for the resilience of the domestic supply chain?
- How can a severe weather event such as Tropical Cyclone Jasper illustrate potential disruption?
- What might different hypothetical supply-loss scenarios mean for available supply and value?

## Key findings

- Australian banana production was **368,701 tonnes in 2024–25**.
- Queensland accounted for approximately **94%** of national production in 2024–25, highlighting the geographic concentration of supply.
- The five-year production series analysed in the workbook shows an overall decline of approximately **8.5%** from 2020–21 to 2024–25.
- The project uses the December 2023 Severe Tropical Cyclone Jasper event as a weather-related disruption case study, including heavy rainfall, flooding and access challenges.
- A **34% supply-loss scenario** is included as an illustrative stress test. It is not a measured loss from Jasper, a forecast or a prediction.

## Data sources

The analysis draws on publicly available information from:

- Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) — *Australian Agriculture: Horticulture, 2024–25* and related regional production estimates.
- Hort Innovation / Freshlogic — *Australian Horticulture Statistics Handbook 2024–25*.
- Australian Banana Growers' Council — industry information and updates.
- Bureau of Meteorology (BoM) — Queensland annual climate information and Severe Tropical Cyclone Jasper event information.
- Queensland Reconstruction Authority — information on the impacts and recovery associated with Tropical Cyclone Jasper.

Please refer to the workbook's source notes for the specific data series and references used.

## Important limitations

- Public production and weather data do not provide retailer-level inventory, deliveries or shelf-availability records.
- The analysis identifies potential supply-chain exposure; it does not prove a specific quantity of lost production or retail stockouts caused by a weather event.
- Regional ABS production estimates are experimental and should be interpreted with the relevant ABS caveats.
- Scenario results are illustrative and depend on the assumptions stated in the workbook. They should not be interpreted as forecasts.
- The historical Cyclone Larry comparison and the national hypothetical supply scenario use different geographic scopes; they should not be treated as directly equivalent.
